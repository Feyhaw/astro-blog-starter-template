One of the two ships involved in [Ernest Shackleton’s](/dictionary/ehshackleton) The Imperial Trans-Atlantic Expedition in 1914. The ship was built in Norway and initially named Polaris. Equipped with coal engines, she was specifically designed to sail in packed ice.[1](#ref-1) She was bought by Shackleton in January 1914. The expedition was privately funded; one of the life boats was named after one major doner Sir James Caird, which eventually saved the crew’s life. Endurance was trapped in sea ice in February of 1915, suffering pressure from the expanding ice. On October 27th, 1915, Shakleton ordered the crew to abandon ship and Endurance was finally crushed and sunk on November 21, 1915.[2](#ref-2) Shakleton and five others left with one boat (Caird) and search for rescue.[3](#ref-3) On March 5, 2022, the wreckage of Endurance was founded during the Endurance 22 Expedition by the ship S.A. Agulhas.[4](#ref-4)
