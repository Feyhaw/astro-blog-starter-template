An organization set up in 1946 to promote peaceful use of nuclear energy. One of its works is to transfer atomic technology to civilian agency. It was later superseded by Nuclear Regulatory Commission in 1975. It once promoted tourism on nuclear tests as an exciting holiday event in the 1950s.[1](#ref-1) In 1954, it oversaw the thermonuclear tests at Bikini Atoll, which caused displacement of local residents.[2](#ref-2) During this time, the atomic energy was exhibited as an unlimited source of energy and friend of mankind.[3](#ref-3) 

Before the 1963 nuclear test ban, the proposal of using nuclear bomb to widen the Panama Canal was seriously considered. Many nuclear power plants were approved after 1965.[4](#ref-4) It is worth noting that Soviet Union actually created a reservoir by detonating a nuclear bomb in 1965. See [Lake Chagan](/dictionary/chagan) for more information.
