 Although there have been hypothesis that land moves, Wegener was the first one to develop a scientific mechanism about it. He first openly discussed his hypothesis of tectonic plate and Pangaea in 1912. In 1915 his book <em>The Origin of Continents and Oceans</em> first presented evidence for such a claim.[1](#ref-1) The continental drift theory was proven by the scientific research during the third International Polar Year, or the International Geophysical Year.[2](#ref-2)
