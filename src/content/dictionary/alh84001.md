ALH84001 is a rock picked up in Far Western Icefield of Allan Hills (Near the Italian Zucchlli Station, 360 km from [McMurdo Station](/dictionary/mcmurdost)) on December 27th, 1984.[1](#ref-1) It was classified as a Martian meteorite, and carries (likely) biogenic chemical trace. It is currently preserved at [NASA](/dictionary/nasa).
