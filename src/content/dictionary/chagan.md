Also known as the Shagan Lake locally, it is an artificial lake/reservoir created by nuclear test on January 15, 1965, in Shagan, Kazakhstan. It was said by various sources that as soon as the lake was formed, the minister of the Ministry of Medium Machine Building, Efim Slavsky, dived into the lake to demonstrate the safety.[1](#ref-1) The Ministry of Medium Machine Building was the equivalence of [Atomic Energy Commission](/dictionary/atomicec) of the USA, it oversaw all the nuclear instalment of USSR, including atomic bomb (i.e. Tsar Bomba) and powerplant (i.e. Chernobyl)
