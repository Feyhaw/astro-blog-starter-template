 One of the representatives of the Heroic Age of Antarctic Exploration. He participated in the Robert F Scott’s 1904 Anatectic Expedition, which built the [Discovery Hut](/dictionary/dhut).[1](#ref-1) In 1908, he built cape Royds hut on Ross Island In 1914 Antarctic expedition, the ship [Endurance](/dictionary/endurance) was trapped in sea ice and eventually crushed, Shackleton led another five men in searching for rescue after being stranded here for four months.[2](#ref-2)  As the expedition had gone south, the Cape Royds hut was ransacked by Shackleton for emergency supply.[3](#ref-3) All 28 members were rescued and returned to the Falkland Islands in June 1916.

 In 1921, Ernest Shckleton passed away on his final Antarctic expedition. He was buried on South Georgia Island, 1400km East of Falkland Islands.
