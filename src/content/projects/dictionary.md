abstract: | 
    As the PhD research proceeds, terms begin to form a network: explorers, objects, scientists, and architectures form stronger bonds and start to explain themselves. The dictionary began as an ever-growing urge to trace recurring abbreviations; now it has grown into an incomplete "encyclopedia." This dictionary, along with the bibliography and polar architecture timeline, has become part of my research infrastructure. And most importantly, like the timeline, which provides the basis for the historiography study of architecture, the dictionary provides the foundation for an epistemological study. It is a spin-off of my PhD thesis.
